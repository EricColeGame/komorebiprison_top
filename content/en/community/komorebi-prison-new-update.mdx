## How to Prepare Before the Komorebi Prison New Update Goes Live

Good preparation happens in the last few days before the Komorebi Prison new update drops, not after. ## Economy, Progression, and Long-Term Planning

The long game after any Komorebi Prison new update is about flexibility, not prediction. Nobody knows exactly how a balance pass will settle, so the players who do best are the ones holding a mix of liquid currency, useful items, and uncommitted upgrade materials. If you can pivot quickly, a nerf to your main method is an inconvenience rather than a disaster.

| Asset Type | Stockpile or Liquidate | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Soft currency | Spend before, hold after | Stored value is often rebalanced |
| Common crafting materials | Stockpile | Demand usually rises with new content |
| Rare, single-purpose items | Hold | Value depends entirely on the new meta |
| Duplicate gear | Liquidate early | Prices tend to fall as supply grows |
| Event-exclusive tokens | Use before expiry | They rarely survive into a new cycle |

A simple rule: if an item only has value because of one specific mechanic, treat it as risky. If it has value because players always need it, treat it as safe. Apply that filter to your entire inventory and you will usually end up keeping the right things.

Progression planning works the same way. Pick one primary goal for the first week after the update — reaching a new area, unlocking a specific upgrade tier, or rebuilding your currency buffer — and let everything else wait. Trying to chase every new system at once is how players burn out and fall behind the people who stayed focused.

## FAQ

### When does the Komorebi Prison new update go live?

Updates are announced through the game's official channels and community hubs rather than on a fixed public calendar. The reliable approach is to watch in-game notices and the community's announcement channels, then plan your session around the maintenance window instead of guessing.

### Will the Komorebi Prison new update wipe my progress?

Full wipes are uncommon, but partial rebalances are not. Ranks, unlock trees, and reward values can all shift, and it is normal for a few systems to translate differently after a patch. Screenshotting your stats beforehand gives you a clear record if something looks wrong.

### What should I do first after logging in?

Claim your login and event rewards, re-check your loadout, and test one old money-making route. Those three steps tell you whether your routine survived the patch, and they take less than fifteen minutes.

### Where can I find trustworthy information about the Komorebi Prison new update?

Start with official patch notes, then compare them against player experience shared by people who actually tested the systems. If a claim only appears in one post and nowhere else, wait a day before acting on it.
