## Reading the Komorebi Prison Release Timeline: Signal vs. Noise

The most useful skill for tracking any release is knowing which signals carry weight. A studio's own store page is authoritative. An anonymous forum post is not. Between those extremes sits a spectrum, and most of the frustration people feel comes from treating low-tier signals as if they were high-tier ones.

| Signal type | Reliability | Why it lands where it does |

Community reports matter here too, but in a specific way. Player experience is excellent at telling you what something feels like, and poor at telling you when it ships. Treat community reports as texture, not as a schedule.
